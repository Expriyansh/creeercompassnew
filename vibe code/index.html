Frequently Asked Questions

Timeline

How long does it take?

The timeline varies by individual and career path. Generally, beginner level takes 3-6 months, intermediate 6-12 months, and expert level 1-2 years of consistent learning and practice.

🚀

Experience

Do I need prior experience?

No prior experience is required! Our roadmaps start from the absolute basics and gradually build up your skills. Each level is designed to be accessible to complete beginners.

🔄

Updates

Are roadmaps updated?

Yes! We regularly update our roadmaps to reflect the latest industry trends, tools, and technologies. The tech industry evolves quickly, and we ensure our content stays current.

💰

Cost

Is it free to use?

Career Compass is completely free! We believe everyone should have access to quality career guidance. All roadmaps, resources, and features are available at no cost.

🎯

Focus

How to stay focused?

Set clear goals, create a study schedule, practice regularly, and don't be afraid to ask for help. Join communities and work on real projects to stay motivated.
